**Holes → Export hole page** turns the selected hole into a page like the ones in a yardage book. **Export all hole pages** makes one PDF with a page per hole.

{{fig shot:holepage | HOLE PAGE | The fourth hole of the sample course. Tee at the bottom, green at the top.}}

## What is on the page

- The hole **turned to play up the page**, tee at the bottom, with a north arrow showing which way that really is.
- **Par** and the **yardage from every tee set**.
- **Ticks across the line of play** every 50 yards, counting back from the middle of the green.
- **Boxes beside hazards**: two numbers, the yards from the tee to *reach* the bunker or pond and the yards to *carry* it. A single number means the hazard is only a few yards deep.
- The **fairway width** where the tee shot is expected to land.
- The **strategy patterns**, if they are showing in the editor.
- A **scale bar**.

## How the distances are found

Hazards are bunkers, waste areas and water within 45 yards of the line of play. For each one, the app measures how far along the line its nearest and furthest parts are from the tee you are playing from. Change the tee set on the scorecard and the numbers change with it.

The landing zone is the first aim point of a dog-leg, or 250 yards down a straight hole. Par 3s have none.
