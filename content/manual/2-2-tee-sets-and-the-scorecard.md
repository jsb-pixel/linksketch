Most courses have more than one set of tees. **Holes → Tee sets…** lets you name and color them for the course.

{{fig tee-box | TEE SETS | Three tees on one hole. Each set plays its own distance to the same first aim point.}}

## How a set plays a hole

List the longest set first. That set tees off from the start of each hole's line of play, exactly where you clicked when you drew the hole.

Every other set gets its own tee: select a hole, and in the Info panel press **Place** beside the set, then click on the course. From its tee, a set plays to the hole's first aim point (or straight to the green on a par 3) and shares the rest of the line.

A set with no tee placed on a hole simply plays from the longest set's tee.

## The scorecard

There is one yardage column per tee set, with Out, In and Total. **Click a column heading to play from that set.** The yardage shown on each hole, and the strategy patterns, then start from that tee.

{{fig shot:scorecard | SCORECARD | The sample course's three tee sets: 3,330, 3,099 and 2,795 yards.}}

Tee markers can be dragged with the Hole tool, and ⌥-clicking one removes it.
