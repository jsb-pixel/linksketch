## Plain pictures

**File → Export PNG** saves the course exactly as drawn, at one, two or four output pixels for each course pixel. Holes, labels and terrain are included if they are showing. Underlays and the satellite map never are.

## The presentation sheet

**File → Export presentation sheet** adds what a drawing needs to be handed to someone else:

{{fig shot:sheet | PRESENTATION SHEET | Title block, the course, a legend of the surfaces used, scale bar and north arrow.}}

- a **title block** with the course name, holes, par, yardage for each tee set and the size of the property;
- a **legend** listing only the surfaces that actually appear;
- a **scale bar** in round yards, and a **north arrow**.

## Text labels

The **Text** tool (A) puts words on the course: click to add a label, drag to move it, double-click to edit. Label sizes are in yards, so they stay in proportion at any export size.

## Course stats

Open **Course stats** at the bottom of the Info panel, or **File → Course stats…** for everything:

- acres of each surface;
- the number of greens, their total and average size in square feet;
- the number of bunkers and the square feet of sand;
- holes, par and total yardage for every tee set.

Everything is counted from what is visible, pixel by pixel, so it matches the drawing exactly. **Export CSV** saves the table for a spreadsheet.
