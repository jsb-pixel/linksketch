## The ruler

The **Measure** tool (M) works like measuring on a map: click, click, click. Each leg shows its length and the total follows the cursor. Double-click or press Enter to finish; Escape clears it. With terrain, the Info panel also shows the change in elevation from the first point to the last.

## Distance arcs

Select a hole and arcs appear across it:

- **From the tee**: 150, 200, 250 and 300 yards. Useful for seeing what a drive has to carry.
- **To the green**: 50, 100, 150 and 200 yards. Useful for placing approach hazards.

{{fig arcs | DISTANCE ARCS | Arcs from the tee (out) and to the green (back), drawn across the line of play.}}

Turn either set off under **View**.

## The yard grid

**View → Show yard grid** overlays a grid every 25, 50 or 100 yards, labelled along the top. Zoomed far in, a faint grid of single pixels appears as well.

## Areas

Select a shape, or a painted area with the magic wand, and the Info panel shows its area. Small areas read in square feet and large ones in acres, the way people talk about greens and fairways.
