A hole in Linksketch is its **line of play**: the tee, any points you would aim at along the way, and the middle of the green.

{{fig hole-line | LINE OF PLAY | A dog-leg par 4: tee, one aim point at the corner, green. Its length is the sum of the legs.}}

Pick the **Hole tool** (O), click the tee, click each aim point, then double-click on the green (or press Enter). The running length shows beside the cursor as you go.

## What you get

- **Yardage**, measured along the line, leg by leg.
- **Par**, guessed from the length: up to 250 yards is a 3, up to 470 a 4, up to 690 a 5. Press 3, 4, 5 or 6 to change it.
- A **number**. Holes are numbered in the order you draw them; change one in the Info panel and it swaps with whichever hole had that number.

## Editing

With the Hole tool, drag any point to move it. Double-click the line to add an aim point, ⌥-click a point to remove it, and press ⌫ to delete the selected hole.

If the course has terrain, each hole also shows its **climb** from tee to green and what it *plays like*: about one extra yard for every three feet uphill.

**View → Point selected hole up** turns the view so the hole plays straight up the screen. Only the view turns; the course itself stays north-up.
