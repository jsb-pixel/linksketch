Because the drawing is in yards, placing it on the Earth is one step: say where its corner is.

**Map → Place course on map** searches for a place (or takes pasted coordinates) and pins the course there. A satellite layer then appears under the drawing, and the status bar shows latitude and longitude as you move.

Decisions worth recording:

- **Imagery is USGS aerial photography by default**, which is public domain: free to trace and free to use commercially. Sharper Esri imagery is offered too, clearly marked as personal use only.
- **Google's maps are not used.** Their terms forbid tracing.
- The course area can now be **resized from any side** without anything moving on the ground.
- The **view can rotate**, so a hole can be turned to play straight up the screen. Only the view turns; north stays north in the file and in exports.
