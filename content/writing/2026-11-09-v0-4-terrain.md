Golf is played on slopes, so the drawing needed a third dimension.

A course can now carry a **height map in feet**. For a course placed on the map, *Terrain → Import real elevation* fetches it from the USGS 3D Elevation Program (public domain, often measured by lidar). For a fictional course you sculpt it yourself with a brush: raise, lower, smooth, flatten.

It shows as soft **hillshade** and **contour lines** over the drawing. Once it is there, other things light up: the status bar shows the height under the cursor, the ruler reports the climb, and each hole shows its rise or fall from tee to green and what it "plays like" (about one extra yard for every three feet uphill).

The app was also renamed **Linksketch**. Files are still `.fairway`, and old saves open as before.
