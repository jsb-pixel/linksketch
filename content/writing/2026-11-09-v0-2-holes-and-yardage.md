A drawing of a course becomes a course when it has holes.

The **Hole tool** works the way you would describe a hole out loud: click the tee, click any aim points, double-click the green. The line of play is measured as you go, and par is guessed from the length (you can change it with the 3, 4, 5 and 6 keys).

Around that:

- A **scorecard** panel with Out, In and Total.
- **Distance arcs** on the selected hole: 150 to 300 yards from the tee, 50 to 200 back from the green.
- **Underlay images.** Drop in a scan or a screenshot, fade it, and set its scale by clicking two points you know the distance between. Underlays are for tracing; they are never exported.
