Until now, the editor *was* the website. It has moved to [/linksketch/app/](/linksketch/app/), and this site has grown around it:

- a [project page](/linksketch/) with what it does and where it stands;
- a [manual](/linksketch/manual/) that explains the ideas, not just the buttons — how scale works, how the shot pattern is modeled, how hazard odds are counted;
- this devlog.

**If you bookmarked the old address,** nothing is lost. Your course is kept by your browser for this site, not for a particular page, so it is waiting for you at the new address.

Two promises about the site itself: there is **no tracking** of any kind, and the editor **never uploads your work**. Courses stay in your browser until you save them to a file.
