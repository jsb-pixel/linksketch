A hole that looks fine on paper can be brutal for a player whose shots spread sixty yards wide. Strategy mode shows that spread on the drawing.

Press **S** with a hole selected. Each leg of the line of play becomes a shot, and each shot gets an **oval** showing where it is likely to finish. The solid ring holds about four shots in ten; the dashed ring about nine in ten.

- Three players come built in — **scratch, 10 handicap, 20 handicap** — each with a longest shot and pattern widths in yards. Edit them per course.
- Patterns are **tilted**. A right-hander's misses to the right tend to come up short and misses to the left run long, so the oval leans.
- A player who cannot reach the target finishes short, and extra shots are added until the green is reached.
- For each shot the app counts **what the pattern lands on**: fairway, green, rough, sand, water, trees. "23% trouble" is a number you can design against.
- Drag an oval's centre to **aim somewhere else**, such as the fat middle of the green instead of a tucked flag.

The model is simple on purpose. There is no wind, slope or roll yet.

Also in this version: change a course's **detail level** (½, 1 or 2 yards per pixel) after the fact, without anything moving.
